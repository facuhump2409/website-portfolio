Method

How I build AI that survives contact with production.

  1. 01

    Evals before features

    If you can’t measure the output, you’re shipping vibes. The harness comes first, and it stays in CI.

  2. 02

    The model is ten percent of it

    Permissions, pipelines, cost ceilings, latency budgets, fallbacks. That is the system. The model is one component inside it.

  3. 03

    Own it all the way to the pager

    Prometheus and Grafana on AI systems, same as any other service. Shipping ends when it’s observable, not when it merges.
